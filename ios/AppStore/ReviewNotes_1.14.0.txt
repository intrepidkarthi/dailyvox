DailyVox 1.14.0 (build 49) — notes for review

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS BUILD

The interface is now available in German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil) as well as English. It follows the app's language in iOS Settings > DailyVox > Language, or the device language. To review it in another language, change either one.

A new setting, Settings > "Language you speak", chooses the language speech is transcribed in, separately from the interface language. The list shows only languages this device can transcribe on the device, now or after a one-time download from Apple. Automatic, the default, behaves as in 1.13.0.

Features that read English text only show nothing for entries in other languages, rather than a wrong reading: the suggested mood, the reflection under an entry, mood trends, themes, personality reads, and search by meaning. Search in other languages matches words. This is intended, not a defect. Ask your Twin answers in the question's language (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese); quoted entries are shown as they were written.

SHARE YOUR SKY

On the 7th, 21st and 42nd day the user has spoken, Today offers, once each, to share a 9:16 picture of their constellation through the standard share sheet. The picture shows a count of nights and the address getdailyvox.com: no entry text, names or moods. It is drawn on the device and the app uploads nothing. Settings > Sharing > "Offer to share milestones" turns the offer off.

SPEECH IS TRANSCRIBED ON THE DEVICE, ALWAYS

Recognition is on-device only (requiresOnDeviceRecognition). If the device has no on-device model for the chosen language, the recording is kept, no transcript in another language is written, and the app explains how to install the model; the user can type instead. That is intended behaviour, not a defect.

ICLOUD SYNC

Off by default for new installs (Settings > iCloud Sync). When on, entries sync through the user's own private iCloud database via CloudKit; DailyVox has no server. The Settings text says the data is encrypted by Apple and end-to-end only with Advanced Data Protection.

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, no accounts. PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares no tracking and no collected data.

HEALTH AND THE "BODY" SHARE CARD (UNCHANGED)

HealthKit data is read only with permission, reviewed by the user before it is kept, and never leaves the device. The Body share card appears only after a snapshot is kept, has its own switch that is off each time the share sheet opens, and never shows heart-rate variability or resting heart rate.

PERMISSIONS USED (ALL OPTIONAL EXCEPT MICROPHONE AND SPEECH)

Microphone: recording entries.
Speech recognition: on-device transcription only.
Notifications: the local daily reminder.
Face ID: optional app lock.
HealthKit: optional body context, reviewed by the user.
Motion: optional "at rest / in motion" tag beside an entry.
Photo library and Apple Music: optional "Your Day" notes (off by default), turned into one-line labels on the device. Photos and audio are never uploaded.

No new permissions or entitlements were added in this build. The microphone and speech purpose strings were reworded and are now shown in the interface language.

TEST ACCOUNT

None required. The app has no accounts and no server. Launch and record.
