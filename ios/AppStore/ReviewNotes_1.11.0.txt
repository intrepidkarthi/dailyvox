DailyVox 1.11.0 (build 44) — notes for review

THE PART MOST RELEVANT TO REVIEW: SPEECH IS TRANSCRIBED ON THE DEVICE, ALWAYS

This build changes requiresOnDeviceRecognition to be unconditional. Previously
the app allowed Apple's server-based recognition when the device was online; it
no longer does. Where no on-device speech model is installed, transcription
fails and explains why rather than falling back to the network. The recording is
still saved and the user can type the entry.

To reproduce on a device with no installed model: record an entry. The app shows
"Recording Saved" with instructions to enable Dictation. That is the fix working
as intended, not a defect.

iCloud sync is user-controlled and off by default for new installs (Settings >
iCloud Sync). When on, entries sync through the user's own iCloud via CloudKit;
when off, nothing leaves the device. The in-app Data Shield panel reports which
of the two is currently true rather than asserting a fixed claim.

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, no accounts.

THE "BODY" SHARE CARD (HEALTHKIT)

New in this build, and the only shareable surface that can carry HealthKit-
derived information. It is gated three ways:

1. It does not appear at all unless the user has reviewed and KEPT at least one
   body snapshot.
2. It has its own switch, which is OFF every time the share sheet is opened.
3. With that switch off, the card renders a "your body stays here" state that
   carries no health value.

It draws sleep hours and the app's own mood score only. Heart-rate variability
and resting heart rate are never rendered on any shareable image.

To reproduce: Settings > Body Twin, keep a snapshot, then Share > Body.

OTHER CHANGES IN THIS BUILD

A Control Center control. "Speak" can be added to Control Centre, the Lock
Screen, or the Action Button. It does not record from there — it opens the app
already recording, because the microphone is only ever live with the app in the
foreground. No background-audio mode is requested.

Live Activity / Dynamic Island. The recording Live Activity shows a 42-second
ring, the phrase currently being transcribed, a mood bar, and Finish / Discard
buttons (LiveActivityIntent, running in the app process). Nothing is transmitted.

Typography. The app now bundles Nunito, Inter and DM Mono (SIL Open Font License
1.1) rather than using the system face. Text scales with Dynamic Type, capped at
1.6x.

Interface. The Speak, Journal, Twin and Entry screens were reworked against the
product's design specification. The Twin's constellation now encodes real data:
distance from the centre is how long ago an entry was spoken, angle is the time
of day, size is its length.

Sharing. New "Tonight" and "Body" cards join the existing share cards. Every
card is rendered on the device and handed to the standard share sheet; the app
never transmits one. Names are excluded by default.

Privacy manifest. PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy ships for the first time in this build.
It declares no tracking, no tracking domains, no collected data types, and one
required-reason API: NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults, reason CA92.1.

PERMISSIONS USED

Microphone — recording voice entries.
Speech recognition — on-device transcription only.
Notifications — one optional local daily reminder, scheduled on the device.
HealthKit (read) — optional body context. Every signal is reviewed by the user
  before the app keeps it. No HealthKit write access is requested.
Photo library — optional, user-initiated attachment and export.

No new entitlements were added in this build.

TEST ACCOUNT

None required. The app has no accounts and no server. Launch and record.
