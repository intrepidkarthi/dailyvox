DailyVox is a **100% offline, privacy-first AI voice diary** for iPhone and iPad. Speak your thoughts, and DailyVox transcribes them on-device — no servers, no cloud, no accounts. Over time, it builds a **Digital Twin** that reflects your personality, emotional patterns, and the people and topics in your life.

**Your thoughts never leave your device.**

## Features

- **Voice Journaling** — Record and transcribe using Apple's on-device Speech framework. Works offline.
- **Digital Twin AI** — Learns your personality, communication style, emotional patterns, and life themes. All on-device.
- **Knowledge Graph** — Tracks the people, places, and topics you mention. See how your world connects.
- **Mood Tracking** — Automatic sentiment analysis with mood trends across days, weeks, and months.
- **Smart Insights** — AI-generated patterns about your journaling habits, writing style, and emotional arc.
- **Face ID / Touch ID Lock** — Your diary is protected by biometric authentication.
- **Photo Attachments** — Attach up to 5 photos per entry, stored on-device only.
- **8 Themes** — System, Light, Sage, Lavender, Rose, Ocean, Warm, and Dark.
- **Encrypted Exports** — Export to PDF, JSON, Markdown, CSV, or AES-256 encrypted backup.
- **Optional iCloud Sync** — Sync across your devices via your personal iCloud. Your choice.
- **Widgets** — Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for streaks, mood, and quick recording.
- **Journaling Goals** — Weekly targets with milestone celebrations to build consistency.

## Privacy

DailyVox has Apple's **"Data Not Collected"** privacy label. Here's what that means:

| What | DailyVox | Most Journal Apps |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| Cloud processing | None — 100% on-device | Audio/text sent to servers |
| Account required | No | Yes |
| Data collection | Zero | Analytics, usage data, often text |
| Works offline | Yes, fully | Partial or no |
| Third-party SDKs | None | Multiple |
| Ad tracking | None | Common |

All AI features — voice transcription, sentiment analysis, Digital Twin, knowledge graph — run entirely on your iPhone's Neural Engine using Apple's NaturalLanguage and Speech frameworks.

## Why I Built This

I've written a diary every day for the past 15 years. The problem was always the same — by the time I sat down to type, half the details were gone. Voice changes that. You speak at 150 words a minute without filtering yourself.

But every voice diary app I tried wanted to upload my recordings to their servers. That was a deal-breaker. So I built DailyVox — everything runs on-device, and the AI that knows you best is the one that never shares you with anyone.
