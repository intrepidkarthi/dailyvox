◆OPEN RESEARCH · PILOT WAVE ONE

Does your Twin actually know you?

DailyVox builds a private model of you — entirely on your iPhone, nothing collected, nothing transmitted. That architecture has a consequence we refuse to dodge: nobody can check the model works by watching a server, because there isn't one. So we measure it the hard way, in the open, and we publish what we find — including the failures. The next measurement needs a handful of real people. Maybe you.

The question

One honest question, precisely stated: how well do the Twin's estimates of your traits agree with how you see yourself? You journal by voice; the model estimates seven things about your style of expression; you answer seven short questions about yourself; we compute the agreement — and compare it against a baseline that guesses blindly. If the model can't beat the blind guess, we say so in public. We've already published one negative result on the developer's own twenty-year diary; the instrument is real.

What taking part involves

01
Journal by voice for a few weeks.About a minute a day in DailyVox, as you normally would. 20+ entries gives the model a fair chance — but any amount is usable.
02
Read the consent note, export your diary.Settings → Export Data → readable text export. Your data stays on your phone until this moment — sharing is a choice you make, not one the app makes.
03
Answer 7 short questions about yourself.Two minutes. There are no good or bad answers — the model is the one being graded, not you.
04
Send both privately to the researcher.Email, AirDrop, or Signal — with the words "I consent". That's it. About ten minutes of your time in total.

What we promise

  • Only numbers are ever published — trait estimates, agreement scores, entry counts, under an anonymous ID like “P3”. Never a word of your diary, never your name, nothing quotable.
  • Your export is read by one named person (the researcher), computed on locally, never uploaded anywhere, never used to train anything.
  • Delete any entries you'd rather not share before exporting — fine, and nobody asks.
  • Withdraw any time before publication; your data is deleted, no questions asked — and in any case when the study concludes.
  • You can see your numbers before anything is published, on request.

Full documents: participant sheet · consent note · the public research kit. This is an independent, unpaid, consented study run by the developer as an independent researcher; it is not a university study.

Join wave one

The first wave is deliberately small — a handful of people, measured carefully, reported honestly. If you journal in English on an iPhone and are 18 or older, you're eligible.

Saw the invitation card in the app? This is that study — the card appears once to journalers with a few weeks of entries, and everything beyond it happens here and over email, never inside the app. Two ways to take part: answer the seven-question self-report and compare it with your Twin's estimates (numbers only, two minutes), or additionally share an encrypted export of your diary for model validation — your choice, spelled out in the consent note before anything moves.
