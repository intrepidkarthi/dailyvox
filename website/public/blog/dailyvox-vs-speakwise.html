DailyVox vs Speakwise: personal journal vs meeting notes

Speakwise shows up in voice-journaling searches, but it is built for meetings, not diaries. It captures conversations, transcribes and summarizes them in the cloud, and syncs to Notion. DailyVox is a personal journal that stays on your phone. If you are choosing between them, you are really choosing between two different needs.

Where Speakwise wins

For capturing in-person meetings, sales calls, lectures, and interviews, then pushing structured notes into Notion, Speakwise is the right tool. It is a work product, and it is priced like one.

Where DailyVox wins

For a private personal journal, where the point is candor and the entries should never leave your phone, DailyVox fits. It is free, needs no account, and builds a Digital Twin from your daily entries instead of summarizing meetings.

The honest line

These solve different problems. Speakwise for work conversations in the cloud. DailyVox for a private personal journal on your device.

Questions

Is Speakwise a journaling app?

It is marketed partly for journaling, but the product is built for meeting and conversation notes, processed in the cloud. DailyVox is a personal journal that runs on-device.

Which is cheaper?

DailyVox is free. Speakwise is a paid subscription that starts around $12.99 a week.
