DailyVox vs Untold: on-device vs cloud voice journaling

Untold and DailyVox both let you speak a journal and get AI insight back. They split on one thing that decides everything else: where the AI runs. Untold sends your entries to the cloud. DailyVox runs on your phone.

Where Untold is the better choice

Untold does more with the cloud. It generates a running "newsfeed about you," writes longer AI follow-ups, and syncs your journal across your devices automatically. If those features matter most, that is its strength. You pay for it in dollars, around $12.99 a month for premium, and in your entries leaving the device to be processed.

Where DailyVox is the better choice

DailyVox is built so the journal never leaves your phone. No account, no subscription. The AI, which it calls the Digital Twin, runs on-device, so you trade some cloud horsepower for a guarantee you can check with a network proxy, and a price of zero.

The honest line

Untold if cloud AI features and cross-device sync matter more to you than where your data sits. DailyVox if a private, free, on-device journal matters more than cloud features.

Questions

Is Untold private?

Untold encrypts your data and stores it in the cloud, with entries linked to your account. End-to-end encryption is listed as a future plan. DailyVox processes everything on-device and collects nothing.

Is DailyVox free?

Yes, free with no account. Untold has a free tier plus a premium plan around $12.99 a month.

Which has stronger AI?

Untold's cloud models can generate more (longer content, a personality newsfeed). DailyVox's on-device Twin trades some of that reach for privacy and zero cost.
