The most honest mirror of yourself.

Your Digital Twin is a mirror built from your own words, not an oracle. It remembers what you've shared and sees patterns you can't. Over time it learns how you think, how you feel and who you mention, and it forecasts your mood from your own past patterns. It even tells you how well it knows you: Twin Resolution is a measured score, not a promise. And it runs on your phone. What is a personal digital twin?

Not a chatbot. Not a prompt tool. A private, evolving model of who you are.the longer you talk, the deeper it sees

Knows how you think

Communication style, formality and reflection depth, measured across every entry.

Knows how you feel

Emotional patterns, mood baselines and triggers, tracked over time with sentiment analysis.

Knows your world

People, places and topics: a knowledge graph of everything that matters to you.

Predicts your tomorrow

Mood forecasting from the timing, language and topics of your past entries.

The constellations within your Twin.

Your Twin isn't one model. It's a set of dimensions: Mind for how you think, Voice for how you speak, Heart for how you feel, and a Graph of who and what you mention. Underneath them all sits a forecast of where you're headed.

From voice to Twin, in five stages.

Every entry flows through the same pipeline on your phone. Pick a stage to see what happens to your words at each step.

Microphone to audio, on the phone.

AVAudioEngine captures your voice from the iPhone microphone and hands it straight to the transcriber. The recording is kept with the entry, on the phone, so you can play it back later.

frameworkAVFoundationformatAAC, 44.1 kHznetworkno calls

Speech to text, on the Neural Engine.

Apple's speech recogniser turns your audio into text with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true, so there is no server round-trip. On iOS 26 the newer SpeechAnalyzer does the same job with lower latency.

frameworkSpeechmodeon-device onlynetworkno calls

Text into signals, locally.

NLTagger extracts sentiment, the names of people and places, and parts of speech, using four Core ML models. Every signal is computed on your phone.

frameworkNaturalLanguagemodels4 Core ML, localnetworkno calls

Signals fold into your Twin.

The Twin engine updates Mind, Voice, Heart and Graph, then recomputes the mood forecast. All of it happens in memory, on the device.

engineTwinEnginedimensions4 + forecastnetworkno calls

Saved to your phone, and only yours.

Core Data stores the entry and the Twin's state in a local database. If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy goes through your own iCloud account. DailyVox has no server of its own to send it to.

Two platforms. Zero vendors.

DailyVox is built on platform SDKs: Apple's frameworks on iPhone, Google and AOSP frameworks on Android. There are no third-party SDKs in the app on either side. Every capability has a home inside the operating system it runs on.

The honest answers.

The questions people ask before they press record. More on the FAQ page.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, with no paywall, no premium tier, no ads and no free trial. The app's source is MIT-licensed on GitHub; the Twin engine it uses is a separate, proprietary package.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No sign-up, no email, no password. Install it and start talking. Your journal lives on your phone, not on our servers, because DailyVox doesn't have any.

What happens to my voice recordings?

They're transcribed on your phone, by the phone's own speech engine, and the audio is stored on the phone with the entry. Nothing goes to DailyVox. On iPhone, if you turn on iCloud sync, your journal is copied through your own iCloud account.

How is this different from Day One or Apple Journal?

DailyVox is voice-first, with on-device AI that builds a Digital Twin of your personality. Day One is cloud-based and text-focused. Apple Journal has no Twin that learns who you are.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing bad happens. One missed day a week won't break your streak, and your Twin picks up right where you left off.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. PDF, JSON, Markdown, CSV, or an AES-256 encrypted backup. Your journal is yours, and you can always take it with you.

A note from the person who built it.

I wrote a diary for twenty years. Then I couldn't any more. So I stopped writing and started talking. That's how DailyVox was born, and why it runs on your phone instead of someone else's server.

Every voice-journaling app I could find shipped audio to a cloud for transcription. I didn't want my most private thoughts processed by a third party, and I didn't want to pay a subscription for it either. So I built my own: free, on-device, with the app's source on GitHub. The Digital Twin is the next step, a model of you that lives on your phone, not on somebody's server.
