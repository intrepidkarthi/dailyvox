Someone shared their sky

A sky made of the nights you kept

DailyVox is a voice journal. Each night you speak to it, a star is added to your sky. About 42 seconds is enough, and you can talk for longer if you like.

The My Sky card shows the shape of that habit: how many nights, and roughly when. It never shows the words. It carries no entry text, no names and no moods.

Nothing leaves the phone unless its owner shares the card themselves. The Android app does not even hold the internet permission, and you can check that in your phone’s Settings.

How to get your sky

Three steps, and the first one is tonight

  1. Install DailyVox

    Free on iPhone and Android. There is no account to make and no email to give.

  2. Speak for 42 seconds

    Say how the day went. It is transcribed on your phone, and the night becomes a star. Miss a night and nothing breaks; the sky simply waits.

  3. Share the card, if you want to

    Your phone draws the card and opens its own share sheet. The My Sky card shows the shape of your nights, not what you said.

Questions

Before you install

Is my journal shared when I share my sky?

No. The card is drawn on your phone and handed to your phone’s own share sheet. DailyVox sends nothing anywhere, because it has no server to send to. Your recordings and entries stay on the device. The privacy page has the details.

What does the sky card show?

The shape of your habit: one star for each night you spoke, and roughly when. The My Sky card carries no entry text, no names and no moods.

Is DailyVox free?

Yes. Every feature, with no paywall, no subscription, no ads and no account. The app is open source under the MIT licence.

Does DailyVox work offline?

Yes. Recording, transcription and the sky all work without a connection. The Android app does not even hold the internet permission; you can confirm that in your phone’s app settings or on the Google Play listing. On iPhone, try it in airplane mode.
