# Consent Note — DailyVox Human Fidelity Pilot

Please read this before sending your materials. Reply **"I consent"** with your submission to take part.

**Who is running this.** Karthikeyan NG, the independent developer of DailyVox, acting as an independent researcher. This is not a university study and has not been reviewed by an institutional review board; it follows the principles below instead, and they are commitments, not boilerplate.

**What you're providing.** (1) Your DailyVox diary text export; (2) your answers to 7 self-description questions, now and once again ~2 weeks later.

**What it's used for.** Solely to compute, on the researcher's own computer, how well the DailyVox Twin's trait estimates from your entries agree with your self-answers. The computation runs locally; your export is not uploaded to any cloud service, not used to train any model, and not shared with anyone else.

**What will be published.** Only numbers, under an anonymous ID (e.g., "P3"): trait estimates (0–1), agreement scores, entry count, and date span. No diary text, no name, no demographic details, nothing quotable or identifying. You can see the exact numbers attributed to your ID before anything is published, on request.

**Your rights.** Participation is voluntary and unpaid. You may withdraw at any time before publication — your data will be deleted and your row removed, no questions asked. Your export file is deleted on request at any time, and in any case when the study concludes.

**Risks.** Minimal, but real enough to state: you are sharing private diary text with one named person. Share only if you are comfortable with the researcher reading it for the purpose above. If there are entries you'd rather not share, delete them from the export file before sending — that is completely fine and does not need to be mentioned.

**Contact.** The researcher, via the channel you received this from, or the support address at getdailyvox.com.
