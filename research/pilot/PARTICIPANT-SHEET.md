# DailyVox Human Fidelity Pilot — Participant Sheet

Thank you for considering this. The whole thing takes about **10 minutes now** and **2 minutes again in two weeks**.

## What this study is

DailyVox builds a private model of you (the Digital Twin) from your voice journal entries, entirely on your iPhone. This pilot measures one honest question: **how well do the Twin's estimates of your traits agree with how you see yourself?**

You give us two things: your diary export (text) and your answers to 7 short questions about yourself. We compute the agreement between the model's estimates and your answers. **Only numbers are ever published — never a word of your diary.**

## What you'll do

**Step 1 — Read and agree to the consent note** ([`CONSENT.md`](CONSENT.md)). Reply "I consent" when you send your materials.

**Step 2 — Export your diary.** In DailyVox: **Settings → Export Data** → choose the readable text export. You'll get a file that begins with "DAILYVOX DIARY EXPORT". (The more entries the better; 20+ gives the model a fair chance — but any amount is usable.)

**Step 3 — Answer the 7 questions below.** Pick ONE option per question. Answer about *yourself as you actually are*, not how you'd like to be — there are no good or bad answers, and the model is the one being graded, not you.

1. **How do you tend to express yourself?**
   Reserved · Somewhat reserved · Somewhat expressive · Expressive
2. **When you say what you mean, you're usually…**
   Nuanced · Somewhat nuanced · Somewhat direct · Direct
3. **You make sense of things more through…**
   Intuition · Mostly intuition · Mostly analysis · Analysis
4. **Your thinking leans…**
   Concrete · Mostly concrete · Mostly abstract · Abstract
5. **Your mind spends more time on…**
   The past · Mostly the past · Mostly the future · The future
6. **When something goes wrong, you tend to feel you can…**
   Accept it · Mostly accept it · Mostly grow from it · Grow from it
7. **Across a week, your emotions are…**
   Steady · Mostly steady · Fairly wide-ranging · Wide-ranging

**Step 4 — Send both privately to the researcher** (AirDrop, Signal, or email — whatever you prefer): the export file + your 7 answers + the words "I consent".

Two practical notes: send your diary as the **export file itself** — please don't paste entries into a spreadsheet or message; and send your **complete history** rather than a sample (the model is graded on your whole run of entries; more is fairer — and you're welcome to delete any entries you'd rather not share before exporting). If you journal somewhere other than DailyVox, a folder of text files named by date (`2026-05-04.txt`) works too — or just ask.

**Step 5 — In about two weeks** we'll ask you the same 7 questions once more. Please don't write down or try to remember your first answers — answering fresh is the point (it lets us measure how consistent *anyone* is with themselves, which is the fair yardstick for the model).

## What we compute and what gets published

- Computed, on the researcher's Mac only: the Twin's 0–1 estimate for each of the 7 traits from your entries, and the agreement with your answers.
- Published (in aggregate, per anonymous participant ID like "P3"): the numeric trait estimates, agreement scores, your entry count and date span. **Never your text, never your name, never anything quotable.**
- Deleted: your export file, on your request at any time, and in any case when the study concludes.

Questions first? Ask before sending anything.
