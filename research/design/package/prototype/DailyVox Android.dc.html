THE ONE HARD UX PROBLEM
On iOS you can say "your Twin runs on the Neural Engine" and be done. On Android the same sentence is false for most phones. So the Twin needs an honest capability tier, surfaced once in onboarding and never nagged again:
Full — Nano present: conversational Twin, cited answers, semantic search.
Standard — no Nano: everything except free-form chat; Ask becomes structured questions answered from your own stats and quotes. Still useful, still honest.
Never — a cloud fallback. It would cost you the only claim competitors can't copy.
WHAT iOS SHOULD TAKE BACK
The airplane-mode proof. The site's strongest argument — "attach a proxy, see zero bytes" — is nowhere in the app. Make it an onboarding step on both platforms: record entry one with the radio off.
Live recording status outside the app. Android gets a Live Update; iOS has Live Activities and a Dynamic Island doing nothing.
Structured Ask suggestions. Built for Android's non-Nano tier, but a blank chat box is a blank page — the thing the whole app exists to avoid. Ship the chips on iOS too.
Capability honesty. iOS has the same split (Apple Intelligence vs not) and currently papers over it.
