THE STRATEGY
DailyVox makes 0 network calls — so every share is user-made, on-device, through the OS share sheet. The constraint IS the campaign.
1 · The Airplane-Mode Challenge. The native viral format: film yourself turning on airplane mode, then journaling. "Now try that with your AI app." Share cards carry an ON AIRPLANE MODE stamp when it's true — provable, remixable, free.
2 · Sky as art. The constellation is the only journal artifact that is beautiful AND private by nature — stars without words. People post art, not diaries.
3 · The receipt. Privacy made funny and concrete: an itemized receipt where everything costs 0. Screenshots like this outperform any feature list.
4 · Year One / Wrapped. An annual on-device "Your Sky, Year One" — the one calendar moment engineered for feeds, computed without a server.
5 · Trust assets as ads. The App Store "Data Not Collected" label, the open-source repo, a network-traffic audit by a YouTuber — marketing you can't buy, only earn.

PRODUCT HOOKS & FUTURE PLAYS
Share flow (in-app): long-press any milestone, sky, or insight → card preview → OS share sheet. Names redacted by default, user toggles them on; card renders on-device; nothing is sent by the app itself. Every card footer: DailyVox ✦ · claim · getdailyvox.com.
Sky wallpaper generator: export your constellation as a lock-screen wallpaper — the brand lives on the user's most-seen surface, privately.
Star exchange (offline-social): hand a friend one chosen quote-card phone-to-phone via QR — no servers, no accounts. Being unable to go online becomes the social gimmick.
Gift a star: the referral card — "I kept 213 stars. Start yours." No tracking links, and the copy says so.
Audit bounty: publicly invite creators to packet-sniff the app. Every failed gotcha video is an ad.
Milestone stamps: nights 42, 100, 365 mint one-time gold cards — scarcity without gamification guilt.
