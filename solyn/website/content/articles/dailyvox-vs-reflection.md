# DailyVox vs Reflection

Reflection and DailyVox both put AI in a journal, then diverge. Reflection is a guided-journaling and AI-coaching app that runs in the cloud and works on every platform. DailyVox is voice-first, runs on your phone, and costs nothing. The choice comes down to coaching and reach versus privacy and price.

| | DailyVox | Reflection |
|---|---|---|
| Core input | Voice | Guided prompts (voice is a feature) |
| Processing | On-device | Cloud |
| Account | None | Required |
| Price | Free | Freemium; ~$250 lifetime |
| Platforms | iPhone | iOS, Android, Mac, Web |
| AI | On-device Digital Twin | Cloud AI coach, guided programs |

## Where Reflection wins

If you want structure, Reflection offers more breadth: a hundred-plus expert-led journaling programs, an AI coach that responds, and your journal synced on every device. That runs in the cloud and behind an account, and the full version is a paid plan.

## Where DailyVox wins

If you want to just talk, keep it private, and pay nothing, DailyVox is built for that. Voice is the core, not an add-on. The Digital Twin runs on-device, there is no account, and there is no subscription.

## The honest line

Reflection if you want cloud coaching, guided programs, and cross-platform reach. DailyVox if you want a free, private, voice-first journal that stays on your phone.

## Questions

**Is Reflection on-device?**
No. Reflection runs in the cloud with an account and cross-device sync. DailyVox processes everything on-device.

**Is voice the focus in both?**
In DailyVox, yes, voice is the core input. In Reflection, voice journaling is one feature alongside typed and guided entries.
