Why Voice Journaling Is Perfect for Public Speaking Prep

Traditional journaling requires time, a desk, and the mental energy to write. Voice journaling needs 2 minutes and a phone. For public speaking prep, this difference is everything — you can journal while walking, driving, cooking, or lying in bed.

DailyVox automatically detects your mood from what you say, tracks the people and topics you mention, and builds a Digital Twin that learns your personality over time. No typing. No internet required. No data leaves your phone.

Getting Started

Download DailyVox (free, no account needed). Press record. Talk about what's on your mind. The AI transcribes your voice, detects your emotional state, and files everything privately on your device. After a few weeks, you'll have a detailed emotional map of your public speaking prep journey that no written journal could match.
