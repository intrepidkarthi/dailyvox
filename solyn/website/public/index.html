how it learns

From voice to Twin, five stages.

Every journal entry flows through the same on-device pipeline. Select any stage to see what happens to your data at each step. (And nothing leaves the phone.)

stage 01 · capture

Microphone to raw audio, locally.

AVAudioEngine captures AAC audio at 44.1 kHz directly from the iPhone microphone. The buffer is held in memory and handed to the transcriber — never uploaded, never cached to disk.

frameworkAVFoundation
formatAAC · 44.1 kHz
latency< 10 ms
networkzero calls

stage 02 · transcribe

Speech to text, on the Neural Engine.

SFSpeechRecognizer turns your audio into text with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true — no server round-trip, in 60+ languages.

frameworkSpeech
modeon-device only
networkzero calls

stage 03 · analyse

Text into signals, locally.

NLTagger extracts sentiment, named entities, and parts of speech using four CoreML models — every signal computed on your phone.

frameworkNaturalLanguage
models4 CoreML, local
networkzero calls

stage 04 · model

Signals fold into your Twin.

The TwinEngine updates every dimension — Mind, Voice, Heart, Graph, Body — and recomputes the mood forecast. All math happens in memory, on-device.

engineTwinEngine
dimensions5 + forecast
networkzero calls

stage 05 · persist

Saved to your device, and only yours.

Core Data stores the entry and the Twin's AIState in local SQLite. Optional sync is end-to-end-encrypted through your own iCloud — never readable by us.

common questions

The honest answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, forever. No paywall, no premium tier, no "free trial." DailyVox is free and open-source.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No signup, no email, no password. Install and start talking. Your data lives on your device, not on our servers — because we don't have servers.

What happens to my voice recordings?

They're transcribed by Apple's speech engine, on your phone. The audio is stored locally. Nothing is uploaded, ever. Run a network proxy during a recording and see for yourself — zero bytes leave the device.

How is this different from Day One or Apple Journal?

DailyVox is voice-first with an on-device AI that builds a Digital Twin of your personality. Day One is cloud-based and text-focused. Apple Journal has no AI. Neither has a Twin that learns who you are.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing happens. No streaks broken, no guilt trips. Your Twin is patient — it picks up right where you left off.

Can I export my data?

Yes. PDF, JSON, Markdown, CSV, or AES-256 encrypted backups. Your data is yours — always portable, never locked in.
