Voice Journaling in Singapore

Singapore's tropical climate is constant: hot, humid, and punctuated by sudden afternoon thunderstorms year-round. There are no seasons to mark the passage of time, which can create a strange psychological flatness -- the days blur together without the seasonal anchors that other cities provide. DailyVox's mood tracking becomes especially valuable here. It gives you markers and patterns in your emotional data that the calendar and weather will not provide.
