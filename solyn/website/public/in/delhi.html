DailyVox works in all of these because transcription happens on your device. For the millions driving on the Ring Road, NH-48 to Gurugram, or the Noida Expressway, voice journaling turns bumper-to-bumper traffic into time well spent.

Delhi's seasons are extreme and deeply felt. The scorching summers, when temperatures cross 45 degrees Celsius in May and June, keep everyone indoors or desperate for AC. The monsoon from July through September brings relief but also flooding and chaos. Winter brings dense fog, bone-chilling cold in January, and the severe air pollution crisis that turns the sky gray for weeks. DailyVox's automatic mood tracking across nine categories gives you an honest picture of how these environmental extremes -- especially the pollution season -- affect your mental state over time.
