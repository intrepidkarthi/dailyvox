Voice Journaling in Stockholm

Stockholm's Tunnelbana is one of the deepest metro systems in the world, with stations carved into bedrock that function as underground art galleries. The Blue Line stations like T-Centralen, Kungstradgarden, and Solna Centrum are beautiful but completely cut off from mobile signals. The Red and Green Lines also have long underground stretches. DailyVox works in every tunnel because transcription happens on your iPhone -- no internet required. For commuters on the Pendeltag from Sodertaljie or Uppsala, or anyone walking across Djurgarden in winter, voice journaling fits seamlessly into the day.

Stockholm's seasons are among the most extreme of any major European city. Winter brings just six hours of daylight in December, with temperatures well below freezing and snow covering the city from November through March. The darkness is real and its impact on mental health is well documented -- Sweden has some of the highest SAD rates in Europe. Then summer arrives with the "white nights" -- nearly 19 hours of daylight in June, when the city moves outdoors and the archipelago comes alive. DailyVox's mood tracking captures this dramatic arc. Seeing your own mood data shift with the light gives you tangible evidence of what the Swedish winter does and what the summer restores.
