Voice Journaling in Bangalore

Bangalore's traffic is legendary. The commute from Whitefield to Koramangala, Electronic City to Indiranagar, or anywhere along the Outer Ring Road can take one to two hours in each direction. The Namma Metro is expanding -- the Purple Line from Whitefield to Kengeri and the Green Line from Nagasandra to Silk Institute -- but most of the city is still car and bus dependent. DailyVox turns that traffic time into journaling time. Speak your thoughts while stuck on Hosur Road or crawling through Silk Board Junction, and the app transcribes everything on your iPhone. No internet needed -- it works in the Metro tunnels and in the dead zones along the IT corridors.

Bangalore's climate is mild compared to Delhi or Chennai, but the city has changed. The once-temperate "Garden City" now faces heat waves in April and May, and the monsoon from June through September brings flooding in low-lying areas like Bellandur and Marathahalli. The city's lakes -- once a cooling network -- have been encroached upon, and the water crisis adds genuine anxiety. DailyVox's mood tracking captures how these environmental pressures, combined with the relentless work culture of the IT industry, affect you emotionally over weeks and months.
