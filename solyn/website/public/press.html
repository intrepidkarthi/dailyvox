DailyVox Press Kit — Facts, Assets, and Story

Everything you need to write about DailyVox · Last updated May 2026

DailyVox is a free AI voice journal with an on-device Digital Twin. It runs 100% on iPhone with zero data collection. Here's everything you need to write about it.

Quick Facts

  • Free voice journal app for iPhone and iPad
  • 100% on-device AI — zero network calls, zero servers
  • Digital Twin: AI personality model with 4 sub-models
  • Apple App Store privacy label: "Data Not Collected"
  • No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads
  • Open source: github.com/intrepidkarthi/dailyvox
  • Created by Karthikeyan NG, a 20-year diary writer
  • First released: March 2026 | Current version: 1.3.0 (Constellation Update)
  • Built with 9 Apple frameworks, 0 third-party dependencies

The Story

Karthikeyan NG kept a handwritten diary for 20 years. When he eventually couldn't maintain the writing habit, he started talking instead — and realized that speaking his thoughts was faster, more natural, and captured more nuance than writing ever did.

He looked for an app that could record voice entries, transcribe them, and analyze patterns with AI — all without sending private thoughts to a cloud server. Nothing like that existed. Every AI journal app he found sent data to external APIs. So he built DailyVox.

DailyVox is free because it has no servers. There is no infrastructure to pay for. There is no recurring cost to pass on to users. And it is open source because privacy claims should be verifiable, not just marketing copy.
